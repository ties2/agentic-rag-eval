Fees and Limits

Standard accounts have a daily transfer limit of 10000 euros and a monthly
limit of 50000 euros. Premium accounts have higher limits set individually
during onboarding.

Domestic transfers are free. International transfers incur a fixed fee of 5
euros plus a currency conversion margin. ATM withdrawals abroad are free up to
three per month, after which a 2 euro fee applies per withdrawal.

There is no monthly maintenance fee for standard accounts. Premium accounts are
billed monthly and the fee is non-refundable once the billing cycle starts.
