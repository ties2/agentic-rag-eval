KYC and Onboarding

Know Your Customer (KYC) verification is required before a new account can send
or receive money. The customer must provide a government-issued identity
document and a selfie for liveness checking.

Accounts that fail verification three times are placed in manual review. During
manual review, incoming transfers are held but the account is not closed.
Verification data is retained for five years to comply with anti-money
laundering regulations.

Politically exposed persons are subject to enhanced due diligence, which
includes a source-of-funds check before the account is activated.
