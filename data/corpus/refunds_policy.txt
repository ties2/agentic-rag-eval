Refunds Policy

A customer may request a refund for a payment within 14 days of the transaction
date. Refunds are only issued to the original payment method. If the original
card has expired, the refund is credited to the customer's primary account
balance instead.

Refund requests for subscription products must be made before the next billing
cycle. Once a new cycle begins, that period is non-refundable, but the customer
may cancel to prevent future charges.

Disputed transactions follow the chargeback process and are not handled under
this refunds policy. A refund is typically processed within 3 to 5 business
days after approval.
