Disputes and Chargebacks

A chargeback is a forced reversal of a card payment initiated by the customer's
bank. Customers should first contact the merchant before opening a dispute. To
open a dispute, the customer files a claim within 120 days of the transaction.

Required evidence for a dispute includes the transaction reference, the date,
the amount, and a short description of the issue. Fraudulent transactions, where
the customer did not authorise the payment, are escalated immediately and the
card is blocked to prevent further loss.

If a dispute is resolved in the customer's favour, the disputed amount is
returned to the account. If it is rejected, the original charge stands and the
customer is notified with the reason.
